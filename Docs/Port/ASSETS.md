Assets ported from movecade-v2 (owned by the project): `kart.glb`, `violet-loop.glb` (confirmed usable by Jimmy
on 2026-10-19), the `kart-*.wav` audio set, and the track data in `Data/Tracks`. `Content/Tracks` holds the
decoded Violet circuit and the scenery, prefab meshes and canvas textures generated from movecade-v2 code by
`Tools/track-export` (no Fab or other third-party assets).

**Environments pass (AAA).** No Fab content and nothing bought. Everything new is original and generated by
code: the Violet Gardens (`src/violetGarden.ts`: terrain, hedges, plinths, pillars and lanterns, the start gate,
crystals, trees, lavender, flower beds, gazebo, conservatory, balloons), Harbor's coast (`src/harborCoast.ts`:
seawalls, rumble kerbs, seabed, palms, dune grass), the shared kit (`src/envKit.ts`) and two seeded textures
(`src/envTextures.ts`: `env_clouds`, the sky's cloud density; `env_waves_normal`, the water's wave normals). The
Violet GLB keeps only its road, line paint (kerbs recoloured violet) and verge; its barriers, start structure,
hills and Mario-style motifs are left out of the visuals and rebuilt as the garden on the same footprints.

The Poly Haven sets above are copied from movecade-v2's own ledger (project-owned copies of CC0 assets); the
exporter converts WebP to PNG and flips the normal maps' green channel (OpenGL to DirectX). Everything else the
maps pass added is original: the Movecade props (`Tools/track-export/src/props.ts`: advertising columns,
billboards, sponsor arches, rock outcrops, plinths, flags, tyre stacks, palms, boost pad), their canvas-drawn
Movecade artwork (`mc_*` textures).

**Violet Loop IP cleanup.** The decoded Violet GLB no longer draws the old "mario-circuit" motifs: the six warp
pipes (`Object_12`), the brick blocks (`Object_47`: stair stacks, floating rows, the gantry feet) and the puffy
clouds are cut from the visual mesh, and the four-colour studded barrier blocks are flattened and repainted
violet/cream. Collision is unchanged (the sim's walls come from the JSON, measured before the cut).

### Vehicles and drivers (art workstream)

All from movecade-v2 `app/public` (project-owned art, ported unchanged apart from decoding):

| Source (`app/public/...`) | UE location | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `models/kart.glb` | `/Game/Karts/Kart/SM_Kart_{body,booster,d_wheel,trim,wheel_0..3}` | Draco decoded; the root's x10 scale and the unused `shape` mesh dropped; wheels rebuilt and `trim` added by the detail pass (below) |
| `game-assets/vehicles/{moto,coupe,truck}.glb` | `/Game/Karts/{Moto,Coupe,Truck}/SM_<V>_*` | Draco decoded; node names kept (`body`, `trim`, `d_wheel`/`handlebar`, `wheel_N`); `seat` read into `AKartPawn`'s mount table; wheels rebuilt, bodies rounded and detail added by the detail pass |
| `game-assets/avatar4/{kai,leo,mira,nia,omar,rose}-{female,male}.glb` | `/Game/Avatars/Drivers/<face>_<body>/SK_Driver_<face>_<body>` on `/Game/Avatars/SK_Avatar4_Skeleton` | Draco decoded; the 65 skinned parts merged into one mesh (one section per material), 26 joint names and 13 morph targets kept; shared materials split per toggle (`hair_<style>`, `acc_*`, `skin_body`, `waist`) |
| `game-assets/clothing/{7 outfits}-{female,male}.glb` | `/Game/Avatars/Outfits/<outfit>_<body>/SK_Outfit_<outfit>_<body>` | parts merged into one mesh, same skeleton |

Pipeline: `Tools/art/decode.mjs` (Node, @gltf-transform 4.2.1 + draco3dgltf, raw output in the git-ignored
`Tools/art/.cache/out`), then `Tools/ue/import_karts.py` (Interchange import, master materials and instances).
Materials are new: `M_KartPaint` (clear coat), `M_KartTyre`, `M_VehiclePart`, `M_NamePlateBack`,
`M_AvatarSkin` (subsurface profile), `M_AvatarHair`, `M_AvatarCloth` (cloth shading), `M_AvatarSurface`.

**Vehicle detail pass.** No third-party assets. `Tools/art/detail/build.ps1` runs a Blender 5.0 script
(`Tools/art/detail/*.py`) over the decoded GLBs and writes `Tools/art/.cache/detail/vehicles` (git-ignored),
which `import_karts.py` imports. Everything it adds is modelled in code: tyres, rims, hubs and brake discs
(`wheels.py`), bumpers, lamps, exhausts, springs, a chain, a fairing, aero parts, a spare wheel and roundels
(`vehicles.py`), and the edge rounding of the stock bodies (`kit.py`). The roundel emblem (a star with a
lightning notch) is an original design. The twelve textures in `/Game/Karts/Materials/Textures` are drawn by
`Tools/art/detail/make_textures.py` (numpy, fixed seeds; PNGs in `Tools/art/detail/textures`):

| Asset | Used for |
|---|---|
| `T_Flake_N` | metallic flakes under the paint's clear coat (`M_KartPaint`) |
| `T_Carbon_C`, `T_Carbon_N` | 2x2 twill carbon weave (`M_KartCarbon`) |
| `T_Brushed_N`, `T_Brushed_R` | brushed steel brake discs |
| `T_Cast_N` | sand-cast hubs and engine cases |
| `T_Plastic_N` | trim plastics and rubber |
| `T_Seat_C`, `T_Seat_N` | quilted, stitched seats |
| `T_Tyre_N` | tyre sidewall rings and tread sipes |
| `T_Lens_N` | prismatic headlamp and tail-lamp lenses |
| `T_Roundel_C` | the racing roundel on bodies and wheel caps |

### VFX (VFX workstream)

No third-party assets: everything under `/Game/FX` is generated from this repo's scripts (`Tools/fx/build_fx.ps1`).

| Asset | Made by | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `T_FX_Star`, `T_FX_Noise`, `T_FX_Chevron`, `T_ItemBox_Question` | `Tools/fx/make_fx_textures.py` (numpy, procedural) | twinkle star, tileable value noise, boost-pad chevrons, the item box "?" glyph; PNGs in `Tools/fx/textures` |
| `M_FX_*`, `M_KartItem`, `M_ItemPrism`, `M_ItemBoxGlass`, `M_ItemBoxLabel`, `M_PP_KartScreen` | `Tools/ue/make_fx_materials.py` | particle, glow, fireball, bolt, pad, skid, item and per-view post-process materials |
| `NS_FX_{Smoke,Fire,Spark,Ring,Flare,Confetti,SpeedLines}` | `Tools/ue/make_fx_niagara.py` (Niagara toolset) | parameterised CPU particle fields |
| Item meshes (shells, banana, bomb, nitro, prism, lightning, ink, rocket, box core and glyphs, lightning strikes) | `Source/MovecadeKart/Private/FX/KartItemMeshes.cpp` | modelled in code at runtime, no asset files |

Engine content used: `/Engine/BasicShapes/{Sphere,Plane,Cube}` (glows, pad flash, skid quads, box shell) and the
Niagara plugin's `DirectionalBurst` / `Minimal` templates the systems are created from.
